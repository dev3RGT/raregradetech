Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before choosing a package or requesting a tailored quote.

How long does a project take?
Most projects take between two and twelve weeks, depending on the package, scope and feedback turnaround. We’ll confirm a realistic timeline before work begins.
Do you offer refunds?
Because work is tailored and delivery begins after approval, refunds are assessed according to the agreed project terms. We’ll make the scope and payment milestones clear upfront.
How many revisions are included?
Each package includes a defined number of revision rounds. Additional revisions can be added to a custom quote when your project needs them.
Can I upgrade my package?
Yes. Packages can be upgraded as your needs grow. We’ll review the remaining scope and recommend the most practical next step.
Do you offer payment plans?
Payment plans are available for qualifying projects and are agreed before kickoff. Ask about milestones when you request your quote.
Do you provide ongoing support?
Yes. Support options range from the included post-launch period to ongoing maintenance and support plans tailored to your needs.
What happens after I purchase?
We’ll confirm your requirements, schedule a discovery session, finalise the project plan and begin the first phase with clear next steps.
Are hosting and domain included?
Hosting and domain registration depend on the package and project requirements. We’ll state all recurring costs clearly in your quote before approval.
